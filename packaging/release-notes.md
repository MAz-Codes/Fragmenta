**First open on Mac and Windows.** These downloads aren't signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate, so your computer asks before opening them.

- **Mac:** drag Fragmenta to Applications and open it. When macOS says it can't verify Fragmenta, click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to "Fragmenta was blocked", and confirm. You only do this once.
- **Windows:** if your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose **Keep**. When Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The first launch downloads Fragmenta's Python packages (up to about 8 GB). Check a download against `SHA256SUMS`.
